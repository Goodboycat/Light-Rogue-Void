You are continuing LIGHT (Light Rogue Void). Read `docs/CONVENTIONS.md` and `docs/HANDOFF.md` first. Credits are scarce: don't re-read files you don't need. All paths are relative to `LIGHT/game/` unless they start with `docs/` or `prompts/` (those are at `LIGHT/`).

## Goal
Half the players are on phones. Make the game run and read well on a mid Android phone, portrait and landscape.

## Read first
`components/MobileControls.tsx`, `components/game/Input.ts`, `components/game/Renderer.ts`, `game/Pools.ts` (4A); fork `components/game/Quality.ts`, `utils/haptics.ts`, `css/mobile-fixes.css` (`bash tools/fork.sh <path>`).

## Steps (commit + push after each: `7B: <step>`)
1. Port `Quality.ts` → `game/Quality.ts`: LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH profiles (particle cap, glow on/off, shadow blur off, damage numbers on/off, DPR cap 1/1.5/2). Auto mode: measure avg frame ms over 3 s windows, step down below 28 fps, up above 55 fps (hysteresis 10 s).
2. Touch: floating joystick (appears where the thumb lands), dash button, Light Burst button, pause; dead zone 8 px; multi-touch safe; no 300 ms delay; no text selection; `touch-action: none` on canvas.
3. Safe areas: `env(safe-area-inset-*)` for HUD and buttons; viewport-fit=cover; landscape layout moves HUD to corners.
4. Haptics: port `haptics.ts`; patterns for hurt, boss phase, burst; setting OFF/LIGHT/FULL.
5. Perf: cull drawing outside view + 64 px; cap DPR; avoid `shadowBlur` on LOW; measure wave 40 for 30 s on a throttled Chrome (CPU 4× slowdown) and record ms in HANDOFF.
6. `docs/MOBILE.md`: checklist (390×844, 844×390, 768×1024), known device quirks.

## Acceptance
