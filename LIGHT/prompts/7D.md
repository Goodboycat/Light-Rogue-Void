## Goal (owner request, logged in `docs/GDD.md` → "World Map · Feel" and "Cinematics")
1. The world map must feel **mysterious**: fog of war over unreached places, revealed as you reach them. **Discovering a new place is epic cinema** (pan/zoom to it, fog burns away, title treatment, music sting).
2. **Storyline cinematics = dialogue that continues into cinematic movement** (camera + actors + fx), not caption-only. Applies to chapter openers, pre-boss lines, boss spawn intros, boss victory outros, the ending. Mid-wave lines, FIELD NOTES, whispers and lore cards stay as non-pausing captions.
