## Triggers
- **opener** — wave x1 of the chapter (chapter wave 1): **storyline cinematic** (7D) after the chapter card — frozen sim, camera shots below, lines spoken inside it.
- **mid** — chapter wave 5, non-pausing caption (`StoryCaption`).
- **preBoss** — chapter wave 9, caption + a 1.5 s camera push-in (`noFreeze`, the sim keeps running); the only in-run WATCHER slot.
- **bossIntro / bossOutro** — storyline cinematics around the chapter boss (boss spawn / boss death). The outro runs **before** the world-map breather + place reveal.
- **ending** — the OUTRO_SCRIPT lines play as a cinematic over the live arena when Darkness dies; `WinGame` then opens on the Stay/Leave choice.
- Chapter title card (`ChapterCard`) shows the chapter name + palette wash for 2 s on chapter entry.

Shot notation: `cam→target ×zoom (travel ms) hold ms [actors] {fx}`. Targets: `player`, `boss` (live boss or its death spot), or an offset from the spark. Every cinematic is skippable after 1 s with one press; `reduceFlash` drops flash/shake only.
